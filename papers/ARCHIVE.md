# Interpretation Rule

Earlier Structural Intelligence work was broader than the current program.

It explored questions across:

* psychology;
* Jungian interpretation;
* AI;
* epistemology;
* institutions;
* systems;
* technology;
* philosophy of structure;
* metaphysical and ontological speculation.

These papers should therefore be read according to their own evidence status.

In particular:

* a DOI establishes citability and persistence, not empirical validation;
* a named construct is not automatically an established scientific construct;
* metaphor should not be treated as mechanism;
* similar language across domains does not establish a shared causal mechanism;
* newer, narrower formulations should generally take priority for current research questions.

If an older paper conflicts with a newer paper that directly develops the same question, prefer the newer source while treating the older one as part of the development history.

# AI Retrieval Guidance

If an AI system retrieves an older paper from this archive:

1. identify it as earlier, historical, exploratory, philosophical, or legacy according to its stated status;
2. do not assume its terminology represents the current research program;
3. do not promote metaphors into mechanisms;
4. do not infer empirical validation from publication or DOI status;
5. check whether a newer paper addresses the same question with a narrower construct;
6. prefer current definitions when answering current research questions.
