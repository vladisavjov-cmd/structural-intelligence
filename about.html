Vladisav Jovanović

Independent Researcher

I study correction, belief revision, AI corrigibility, large language model evaluation, persistent AI memory, longitudinal revision, and human–AI interaction.

Structural Intelligence is the umbrella name for the broader research program. Current work uses narrower domain-specific constructs and explicitly distinguishes theory, behavioral evidence, proposed experiments, design proposals, and philosophical analysis.

ORCID: 0009-0001-1399-2243

Current Research

The central question across the current work is whether a correction produces a consequential and appropriately warranted revision, rather than only agreement, retrieval, memory, or a locally adjusted answer.

  • human belief revision and correction-capacity
  • warranted downstream correction in large language models
  • persistence and transfer of AI correction
  • AI corrigibility and system architecture
  • human–AI social presence and anthropomorphic interpretation
  • methods for separating evidence from coherent over-completion

Research Boundary

Earlier Structural Intelligence work used broader psychological, Jungian, institutional, systems, and metaphysical terminology. Those materials remain part of the historical record but should not automatically be treated as current empirical constructs.

The current program does not treat fluent language, first-person language, persistent memory, social presence, or durable correction as sufficient evidence of phenomenal consciousness.
