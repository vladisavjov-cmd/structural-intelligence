Independent Researcher

Vladisav Jovanović

Also written as Vladisav Jovanovic

I study correction, belief revision, AI corrigibility, human–AI interaction, and the difference between a system changing its current answer and changing what it does later.

Structural Intelligence is the umbrella name for the broader research program. Current work uses narrower, domain-specific constructs and distinguishes theoretical proposals, behavioral evidence, design proposals, and philosophical analysis.

Current Research

Main research questions

My current work asks how a person or AI system should respond when new evidence challenges an existing claim, belief, interpretation, or decision.

The central distinction is between merely producing a corrected answer and carrying justified revision into later reasoning, behavior, decision-making, or system state.

Current research areas include belief revision, warrant-responsive rationality, large language model correction, persistent AI memory, longitudinal AI evaluation, AI corrigibility, human oversight, social presence, anthropomorphism, and human–AI interaction.

Research Boundary

What the current work does not assume

Coherent language is not treated as proof of truth. Agreement is not treated as proof of learning. Memory is not treated as proof of durable correction.

Social presence, first-person language, apparent reflection, persistent memory, or corrective continuity are not treated as sufficient evidence of phenomenal consciousness or personhood.

Earlier Structural Intelligence work used broader psychological, institutional, Jungian, systems, and metaphysical terminology. Those papers remain part of the historical corpus but should not automatically be treated as current empirical claims.

Research Practice

AI-assisted research disclosure
