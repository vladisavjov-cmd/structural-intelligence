Vladisav Jovanović

Independent Researcher

Also written as: Vladisav Jovanovic

Vladisav Jovanović is an independent researcher working across Structural Intelligence, psychology, Jungian thought, artificial intelligence, epistemology, philosophy of mind, human-AI interaction, and AI governance.

His research focuses on correction, answerability, burden, fixed worth, corrigibility, conceptual provenance, and the conditions under which persons, institutions, and AI systems remain revisable under pressure.

Not to be confused with Vladislav Jovanović.
