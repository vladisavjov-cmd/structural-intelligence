# Structural Intelligence

## Research on Correction, Reasoning, and AI

**Structural Intelligence** is the umbrella name for an independent research program developed by **Vladisav Jovanović**.

The current work focuses on a narrower question:

> What distinguishes a real correction from agreement, memory, compliance, or a locally changed answer?

The research examines this question in human reasoning, large language models, AI system design, and human–AI interaction.

---

## Current Research

### Human Correction and Rational Revision

How should a person respond when a belief or judgment is challenged?

The relevant issue is not simply whether correction is accepted.

A rational response should track the quality of the evidence.

Current constructs include:

- **Correction-Capacity**
- **Warrant-Response Calibration**
- **Warrant Discrimination**
- **Calibrated Correction**
- **Reasoned Non-Uptake**
- **Suspended Judgment**

Current paper:

**The Correction-Capacity Model: Warrant-Responsive Rationality Under Self-Relevant Threat**

[SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7320318)

### Warranted Downstream Correction in AI

Changing one answer after a correction does not necessarily show that an AI system has incorporated that correction into later reasoning.

**Warranted Downstream Correction (WDC)** asks whether a justified correction selectively changes claims that materially depend on the corrected premise while preserving independently supported claims.

Current paper:

**Beyond Changing the Answer: Warranted Downstream Correction in Large Language Models**

[SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7373458)

DOI: `10.2139/ssrn.7373458`

### Corrective Continuity

Persistent AI memory can make a system appear continuous across interactions.

But remembering a correction is not necessarily the same as remaining changed by it.

**Corrective Continuity** asks whether warranted correction:

1. persists after the original cue is removed;
2. transfers to relevant new cases;
3. changes materially dependent reasoning;
4. leaves a trace that can account for later change; and
5. remains open to further revision when better evidence appears.

Current paper:

**The Corrective Continuity Hypothesis: Perceived AI Consciousness, Persistent Memory, and Trace-Bearing Revision**

[SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7326338)

DOI: `10.2139/ssrn.7326338`

### Corrigible AI Architecture

A model can produce a corrected sentence without the larger system becoming meaningfully more corrigible.

Current work therefore distinguishes:

- **Prompted Corrigibility**
- **Architectural Corrigibility**
- **Correction Trace**
- **Contact Gate**
- **Non-Continuation State**
- **Re-Revisability**

Current paper:

**Answerability Architecture for Corrigible AI Interaction: Corrigible Continuation Without a Self**

[PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/JOVAAF)

### Human–AI Interaction

Fluent AI systems can produce experiences of:

- social presence;
- recognition;
- continuity;
- apparent understanding;
- relational significance.

Those effects are psychologically relevant.

They are not, by themselves, evidence of machine consciousness, personhood, or subjective experience.

Related current work includes:

**The Machine That Seems Awake: Anthropomorphism, Social Presence, and Evidence for AI Consciousness**

[PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/JOVTMT)

and:

**Psyche-Like Effects Without a Psyche: Coherent Continuation, Borrowed Self-Position, and the In-Between Field of AI Interaction**

[PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/JOVPEW)

## Research Method

The current program emphasizes:

- observation before interpretation;
- explicit evidence status;
- comparison with alternative explanations;
- narrow claims;
- operational definitions where possible;
- downstream consequence testing;
- persistence and transfer testing;
- falsification conditions;
- re-revisability;
- separation of metaphor from mechanism.

A coherent explanation is not automatically a true explanation.

Agreement is not automatically learning.

Memory is not automatically revision.

Persistence is not automatically consciousness.

## Research Boundaries

The current program does not assume that:

- fluent language establishes consciousness;
- first-person language establishes a self;
- persistent memory establishes subjective continuity;
- social presence establishes personhood;
- correction automatically constitutes learning;
- accepting correction is always rational;
- resisting correction is always irrational;
- similar descriptions across domains demonstrate one shared causal mechanism;
- metaphor establishes mechanism;
- philosophical possibility establishes empirical fact.

The strength of a conclusion should not exceed the strength of its evidence.
